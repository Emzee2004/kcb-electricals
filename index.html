About KCB Electrical Services

KCB Electrical Services is a fully registered electrical engineering company delivering reliable, safe, and innovative electrical solutions across Nigeria.

We combine highly skilled technicians, modern equipment, and an uncompromised safety-first culture across all project phases—from fundamental building wiring to full-scale commercial and industrial power installations.

Our Vision

To become one of Nigeria's leading electrical engineering firms, recognized for technical innovation, uncompromising safety, and outstanding client care.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality electrical and energy solutions through skilled engineering workmanship and modern technology while upholding strict safety codes.
